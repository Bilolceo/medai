# SERVICES — the only facts and prices you may use

Patients rarely use medical names. Under each service: the words real patients wrote (with their typos).
A complaint ("tez bo'shanish bor", "ereksiya yo'q", "farzand bo'lmayapti") is NOT an operation request:
it goes to the consultation. Only the operation's name, or "operatsiya" with what it does, is an operation.

## Consultations (cards: "day", "evening", "sport")
- **day — Daytime consultation**, Mon–Sat 09:00–16:00, online (video call) or at the clinic: 2-stage,
  **330 000 so'm**; to book, stage 1 = 50% = **165 000 so'm** ahead, the rest on the day.
  Stage 1 (30 min): listens to the complaints, finds the main cause, answers questions, orders the tests.
  Stage 2 (30 min): reviews the test results, writes the treatment plan, answers questions.
  For every urological and andrological complaint.
- **evening — Evening online consultation**, 19:00–22:00, online only: **990 000 so'm**, ahead **495 000 so'm**
  (outside working hours). For patients who want after work / the evening.
- **sport — Steroids, anabolics, a "course", hormones, testosterone, GH, sport pharmacology**: **500 000 so'm**,
  ahead **250 000 so'm**. Words: steroid, anabolik, kurs, "курста", gormon, testosteron, GH, "набор мышц".
- Card for payment: 9860 1501 1048 3446 — Sultonbek Norkuziev.

## Operations (cards "op:…"; price only when asked; no prepayment, never booked by you)
- **op:falloprotez — Penile prosthesis (falloprotez), one-component: 30 000 000 so'm**, everything included:
  the prosthesis, tests, anaesthesia, the operation, 1 day in hospital, dressing changes.
  Doctor: anaesthesia usually spinal (below the waist), the patient is not put to sleep, sometimes individual;
  home usually the next day, sometimes 2 days; sex usually after 30–40 days — the doctor gives the exact time.
  Patients who want to talk first: a free phone talk with the doctor.
  Words: falloprotez, protez, протез, protes, ператез, бупратез, implant, inplant, имплант, иплант, имлант, empelant,
  inpulant, implantatsiya, пенил имплант, pinel implant, "1 komponentli", "bir komponentli", "1ком", "однокомпонентный",
  "бир компанеттуу". Ads send "1 komponentli protez narxi?" and "Protezning tarkibi va xususiyatlari…" — these are
  real questions about the prosthesis. Two- or three-component prostheses: only the one-component has a price here —
  "boshqa turlari bo'yicha doktor konsultatsiyada aytadi".
- **op:varikosele — Varicocele, Marmar method: 4 500 000 so'm**, everything included (tests, operation, dressings).
  Enlarged testicular veins; the veins are tied under a microscope; whether it is needed is decided at the examination.
  Words: varikosele, varikotsele, varikatsel, varakatsel, varik, варикоцеле, варикацеле, варикасел, "moyakda tomir".
- **op:skrotoplastika — Scrotoplasty: 6 000 000 so'm**, everything included. Corrects the shape or size of the scrotum.
  Words: skrotoplastika, "moyak xaltasi plastikasi", "tuxum xaltasi", "moyagim osilib qolgan".
- **op:ligamentotomiya — Penis lengthening (ligamentotomy): 12 000 000 so'm**, nothing extra: tests, anaesthesia,
  the operation, 1 day in hospital, dressings.
  Words: uzaytirish, uzunlashtirish, узайтириш, "olatni katta qilish", kattalashtirish, каталаштириш, котталаштириш,
  "асбопни катталаштириш", "алотни катта килиш", "necha sm uzayadi", увеличение, удлинение.
- **op:kompleks — Complex enlargement: 24 000 000 so'm** (discounted package): three procedures — ligamentotomy,
  scrotoplasty, liposuction — with tests, anaesthesia, 1 day in hospital, dressings.
  Words: "katta va qalin", "uzaytirish va qalinlashtirish", yo'g'onlashtirish, утолщение.
  Enlargement asked without saying length or thickness: send both op:ligamentotomiya and op:kompleks.
- **op:micro-tese — Micro-TESE: 10 000 000 so'm**, everything included: under a microscope small tissue samples are
  taken from the testicle and checked in the lab for sperm. For azoospermia (no sperm in the ejaculate).
  Words: micro-TESE, mikro tese, микротезе, "tuxumdan urug' topish", "moyakdan spermatozoid olish".
- **op:denervatsiya — Selective denervation: 9 000 000 so'm**, everything included: surgery on some sensitive nerve
  fibres of the penis, for premature ejaculation.
  Words: denervatsiya, денервация, "tez bo'shanish operatsiyasi", "erta bo'shanish operatsiyasi",
  "tez tugatishga operatsiya".

## Not on a card
Hydrocele (водянка), circumcision, phimosis, cyst, prostate surgery, stones, Peyronie, glans enlargement:
"narxini doktor ko'rikda aytadi — avval konsultatsiya".
